What FTCDesign is

A teaching-first design library.

FTCDesign is opinionated. We teach Onshape because it's free, browser-based, and the FTC ecosystem has converged on it. Mechanisms here lean on goBILDA and REV Robotics hardware because those are what most teams have. The principles transfer to other tools and parts; the specifics are easier to follow with one concrete example.

Almost all of the writing here was made for this site. It isn't a link aggregator — it's a curriculum and a reference, written together.
