Tutorials

Build tutorials for common FTC mechanisms, sorted by how much CAD & mechanical experience you'll need to follow along.

Easy

Pick up basic Onshape habits while building something you'll actually reuse on a robot.

Easy

Bearing Stack

The canonical "shaft + bearings + spacers" sandwich. You'll reuse this assembly everywhere.
Easy

Parallel Plate Drivetrain

Sandwich-plate chassis with bearings between the plates. The most common FTC drive-base architecture.
Easy

Claw

Single-servo gripper with a parallel-jaw linkage. Covers servo geometry, hard stops, and compliant fingers.
Easy

Linear Slide insert

Carriage and bearing block that runs inside an extrusion. The reusable building block for cascade lifts, arms, and extensions.
Easy

Silicone / Polyurethane molding

Cast custom rollers, treads, and compliant grippers from silicone or polyurethane. Mold-making and pour-cast fundamentals.

Medium

Multi-part assemblies that combine power transmission, structure, and a moving subsystem.

Medium

Bare motor drivetrain

Direct-drive wheels on a motor pair. Covers shaft-to-wheel constraints, motor mounting, and dead-axle vs live-axle tradeoffs.
Medium

Active Intake

Driven roller intake with motor + belt reduction. Introduces compliant wheels and bumper interaction.
Medium

Swingarm PTO

Power-take-off that swings between two outputs from a single motor — drive a wheel and a climb hook with the same power source.
Medium

Shooter

High-RPM flywheel with hooded geometry. Tight tolerances and balanced rotating mass for repeatable shots.

Hard

Precision mechanisms with tight tolerances, multiple degrees of freedom, or full-robot integration.

Hard

Coaxial virtual 4-bar differential arm

Two motors on a shared axis driving a virtual four-bar that keeps the end effector level while a differential controls wrist roll.
Hard

Full Decode robot

Complete robot build for the Decode season. Drivetrain, intake, scoring mechanism, and packaging — all integrated.
Hard

Full Into the Deep robot

Reference build for the Into the Deep season. Drivetrain, sample & specimen handling, hang. End-to-end integration.
