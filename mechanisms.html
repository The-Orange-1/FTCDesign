Mechanism Examples

Designs that work, with the trade-offs called out.

Five categories. Each example is a real mechanism with a forkable Onshape document and an opinion attached — what it's good at, where it fails, and what to copy.

Drivebases

The chassis carries everything else. Pick this first; everything else flows downstream.

Mecanum drivebase, 14″ square

MecanumgoBILDA 5202 · 19.2:1

A balanced first-year drivebase. Strafes cleanly, holds position under contact, leaves room above for a vertical lift.

Tank drive with traction wheels

TankREV HD Hex

When pushing matters more than strafing, two large traction wheels per side beat any mecanum setup. Trade-off: every position adjustment is a three-point turn.

Intakes

How a piece gets into the robot. Game-piece dependent — patterns transfer, dimensions don't.

Active compliant-wheel intake

ActiveStefan compliant wheels

Two or three compliant wheels, geared together, pull pieces in and hold them. Forgiving on alignment — the wheels deform to fit.

Under-the-bumper claw

Passivesingle servo

A geared parallel-jaw claw driven by one servo, mounted low so it ducks under field elements. Repeatable grip, fixed opening width.

Game-piece manipulation

What happens between intake and scoring — indexing, controlling orientation, releasing on cue.

Belt-driven indexer

Indexer

A timing-belt indexer that moves pieces single-file from intake to release. Predictable spacing; one motor.

Servo flap end-effector

End effector

A servo-driven flap that holds a piece against a back wall, then releases. Simple, fast, and deterministic.

Linear extensions

The reach problem. Cascade, continuous, and telescope rigging — each with a different speed/length tradeoff.

Cascade slide, 3-stage

Cascadeviper slide

Three nested aluminum extrusions, rigged so all stages move together. Reaches further than a continuous slide of the same retracted length.

Continuous-rigged slide

Continuous

One motor drives all stages via a single continuous loop. Faster top-stage motion than cascade; less total reach for the same retracted length.

Rotating mechanisms

Pivots and turrets — anything that swings under load.

Virtual four-bar arm

Pivottwo motors

Two parallel links keep the end effector level through the full sweep. Lighter than a vertical lift; fixed reach radius.

Slewing-ring turret

Turretadvanced

A 360° rotating platform on a slewing ring. Rare in FTC and high-payoff when the game justifies it.
